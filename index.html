The person behind the commits

I'm Kelvin, a graphic designer and front-end developer who likes problems with a right answer hiding in the data — or the brand — somewhere. My work splits into two halves that keep feeding each other: visual identity, meaning logos, brand systems, and print and social collateral, and the websites and dashboards that carry that identity online.

I care about clarity over decoration. Before I open a design file or a code editor, I want to know what the business is trying to say and who needs to understand it. That habit sits behind everything a few pages down: a business-website brief, a couple of data dashboards, and — because not everything has to be client work — a playable chess board.

05Process

How a project moves

  1. Start

    Understand the data

    Before any UI, I figure out what question the screen needs to answer, and for whom.

  2. Then

    Sketch the structure

    Wireframes and component maps, so layout decisions happen before pixels do.

  3. Then

    Build in the open

    Working commits, visible progress, early previews rather than a single reveal.

  4. Finish

    Test & hand off

    Cross-device checks, an accessibility pass, and clear notes for whoever maintains it next.
